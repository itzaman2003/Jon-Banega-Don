**The Council of 37 AIs** is an over-engineered parliamentary simulation designed to resolve trivial human dilemmas—such as replying *"haha"* vs *"lol"*, closing 87 browser tabs, or drinking chai vs coffee—by convening an absurd committee of 37 artificial intelligence delegates.

---

```text
QUESTION UNDER REVIEW:
"Should I reply 'haha' or 'lol'?"

[Dr. Alistair Vance - The Rationalist]
"We need a statistically significant sample of 4,200 prior interactions."

[NullPointer_0x42 - The Chaos Agent]
"Reply with 'ha' to assert conversational dominance."

[Victoria Sterling - The Risk Officer]
"Potential reputational damage and emotional liability detected."

[Harold Penndleton - The Bureaucrat]
"I propose we schedule a caucus to define 'haha' under Triplicate Form 88-B."

FINAL RESOLUTION:
DECISION DEFERRED (INDEFINITELY).
The Council mandates 3 new subcommittees and a 72-hour recess.
